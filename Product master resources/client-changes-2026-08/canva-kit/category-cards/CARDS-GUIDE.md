# Canva guide — 5 category card images (4 products each)

Folders `1-automotive` … `5-grease` hold the product cutouts, file names give
the left→right position. All product photos have pure-white backgrounds now,
so Canva's BG Remover will cut them perfectly in one click.

## Ground rules

1. **Canvas: 1600 × 1200 px (4:3) for every card** — that's the exact shape
   the cards display at, so nothing gets cropped. Set your background to FILL
   the canvas (its sides will crop off — fine).
2. BG-remove every product, never stretch (corner handles only).
3. One baseline: bottoms aligned at **y ≈ 1130** (≈70 px above the bottom
   edge). Exception — **agricultural**: products stand ON the wooden table,
   baseline ≈ **y 1020**.
4. Slight 10–20 px overlaps; the two CENTER items in front.
5. Same soft drop shadow on all products (transparency ~25, blur ~25).
   The three dark backgrounds (automotive garage, grease bay, industrial hall)
   hide shadows well — still add them for grounding.
6. Note: the site overlays a subtle dark gradient at the card's bottom for the
   label — keep product labels roughly in the middle of the product, not at
   its very bottom.

## Height cheat sheet (for the 1200 px canvas)

drum ≈ **430 px** · pail ≈ **380 px** · grease bucket ≈ **380 px** ·
5 kg bucket ≈ **300 px** · 5 L can ≈ **290 px** · 3 L can ≈ **260 px** ·
1 L bottle ≈ **200 px**
